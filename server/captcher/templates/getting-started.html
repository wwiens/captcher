Your first walkthrough

Getting started

Perform a task once, and Captcher turns it into a walkthrough someone else can watch and then practice. Five steps from recording to sharing.

01 · Record 02 · Author 03 · Try it 04 · Edit 05 · Share

First, connect the recorder

Use desktop Chrome and install the extension. Open its side panel on a Captcher tab like this one; it connects by itself and shows Connected.

Set up the Chrome extension

Step 01 / Record

Show one task from start to finish

Pick a small task, such as finding a customer record or logging a call. Start on the screen a learner would see first.

  1. Open the application you want to teach and the Captcher Recorder side panel.
  2. Enter a clear walkthrough title and click Start recording.
  3. Perform the task in order. The recorder captures the screens and actions as you work.
  4. Click Finish & submit in the panel and wait for the upload to finish.

Check: open Walkthroughs. Your recording appears as a draft in the Authoring queue.

A recording can be up to __MAX_STEPS__ steps and __MAX_PAGES__ different pages; record a longer task in parts. Recording happens in the live application, so use sample data and a task you are comfortable performing.

Step 02 / Author

Let AI write the walkthrough

  1. Find your draft in the Authoring queue on Walkthroughs.
  2. Click Author with AI. Captcher checks that each captured screen renders, then writes the steps, instructions and hints from your recording.
  3. Wait for authoring to finish. The banner above the queue shows what it is doing, and Stop ends the pass.

Check: the walkthrough leaves the queue and appears in your library.

Authoring calls the Anthropic API with your own key, so each pass is billed to your Anthropic account. Settings shows whether the key is set and which file it goes in.

Step 03 / Try it

Check both ways to learn

  1. Click the walkthrough's title in your library. Choose Start to watch it in Show me: each step plays with its caption.
  2. Switch to You try at the top and do the task yourself on the captured screens. Each instruction appears beside the part of the screen to use, and Hint is there if you need it.
  3. Work through to the end. Make sure each instruction matches the screen and each action moves on correctly.

Check: a new learner could complete the task without you explaining what to do.

The player uses captured copies of the screens, so practicing here does not change the live application.

Step 04 / Edit

Make the instructions your own

  1. In Walkthroughs, open the card's More options menu and choose Edit.
  2. On the Step tab, select a step and adjust its Step title, Instruction for You try, Caption for Show me, or Hint. Play step previews it.
  3. Click Save, then play the walkthrough again to check the result.

Check: the wording is clear and the walkthrough still works from start to finish.

For a bigger change, open the AI assist tab and describe it in plain words, or ask a question about the walkthrough. Every change can be undone.

Step 05 / Share

Choose how learners will open it

On the walkthrough card, open More options → Download…. Choose a brand and a format, then click Download.

  1. Single HTML file: share one file that learners can open in a browser.
  2. Folder (.zip): get an HTML page and its supporting assets together.
  3. SCORM 2004 or SCORM 1.2: upload the ZIP to your learning management system, using the version it supports.

For a website, choose More options → Embed… instead. Click Enable embedding, then Copy code and add it to your site.

Check: open the download, or test the walkthrough where learners will use it. Enabling embedding makes that walkthrough publicly accessible.

Downloads play offline, with no server required, and you can download again as often as you like.
